## Summary

Gang is a <strong>Red Hat Certified Architect</strong> with more than fourteen years of experience across <strong>higher education research IT</strong>, <strong>data center operations</strong>, <strong>infrastructure procurement and capacity planning</strong>, Linux and Windows systems, networking, storage, automation, information security, <strong>HPC/GPU computing</strong>, and modern platform engineering. He also applies AI to practical workflows through <strong>governed GPU model serving</strong> and <strong>modular AI agents with reusable skills</strong>, human approval, and auditability.
